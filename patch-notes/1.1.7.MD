# ActionLITE v1.1.7 Release Notes

Version 1.1.7 | November 6, 2026

This cumulative release includes the previously documented v1.1.0 Healing Rotation foundation and v1.1.5 pre-launch update, followed by v1.1.6 and v1.1.7 changes.

## v1.1.6 startup hotfix

- Fixed a Lua 5.1 load failure that prevented the main icon, controls and menu from appearing. The spell-watchdog state now stays within Forever's function upvalue limit.

## Included v1.1.0 Healing Rotation foundation

### Shared HealingEngine

- Added the WoW Forever HealingEngine foundation for Druid, Paladin, Priest and Shaman.
- Added the initial **Healing Rotation** gate to each healer class. In v1.1.5 this is controlled by the shared **Heals** role, with **DPS** selected by default.
- Added **Initiate healing at HP**, default 80%, for efficient heals and HoTs.
- Added **Emergency healing**, default 30%, for large heals and emergency cooldowns.
- Added protected friendly-unit selection through the Action-compatible focus pixel.
- Added protected predicted-health masks so the healing spell, A[3] recommendation and focus target use the same client-evaluated condition.
- Added nested health and mana masks for multi-member heals without reading protected health or mana in Lua.
- Added readable eligibility checks for group membership, role, connection, visibility, death and confirmed spell range.
- Added Healing Rotation state and decisions to ActionLiteDebug reports.
- Selecting a living friendly dungeon-party member is treated as a distress signal and immediately requests the provider's strongest normal direct heal.
- Healing takes priority over healer damage. When healing is not needed, wand users use timed Shoot and non-wand healers may apply a periodic damage spell above 80% mana.
- Added a shared five-second mana rule. After a successful healing or support cast, offensive recommendations pause so Spirit regeneration can begin; required healing remains available below the selected threshold.
- Known DPS members use both healing thresholds at ten percentage points below the selected values. Tanks and the healer keep the full configured thresholds.
- Disabling Healing Rotation, leaving an eligible dungeon or entering unsupported content clears the focus pixel and returns to Leveling Rotation.

### Druid Healing Rotation

- Added tracked Rejuvenation, Regrowth and Healing Touch priorities.
- Added a tracked Rejuvenation follow-up after Healing Touch or Regrowth when the Druid's own Rejuvenation is not already active on that member.
- Added emergency Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Healing Touch and Elune's Light priorities.
- Added tracked Thorns maintenance on the dungeon tank.
- Added out-of-combat Mark of the Wild and health top-off for a selected friendly member while mana is above 80% and the Druid is not eating or drinking.
- Added timed Faerie Fire on a selected elite enemy and timed Moonfire above 80% mana.
- Added Wild Growth for two eligible injured dungeon-party members with a protected 50% mana floor.
- Swiftmend requires one of the casting Druid's tracked Rejuvenation or Regrowth effects. A confirmed Swiftmend conservatively clears both tracked HoTs on that member when the consumed aura cannot be read.
- Dispels, Entangling Roots, Hibernate and Tranquility remain manual.

### Priest Healing Rotations

- Added mutually exclusive **Discipline Priest** and **Holy Priest** healing choices. If neither is selected, Discipline is used as the safe default while Healing Rotation is enabled.
- Discipline maintains tracked Power Word: Shield, shields an injured member before its filler heal, and uses Penance at 20 percentage points below the initiate-healing threshold.
- Discipline uses Flash Heal as its filler and limits Power Infusion to an eligible caster DPS member.
- Discipline reserves automatic Prayer of Healing for an Inner Focus emergency sequence; ordinary Prayer of Healing remains manual.
- Holy tracks Renew, uses Heal for smaller injuries and Flash Heal for larger injuries.
- Holy emergency healing prefers Prayer of Mending, then Inner Focus with Greater Heal.
- Added automatic learned Priest racials: Divine Grace, Elune's Light, Eureka!, Dark Sacrifice, Berserking and Rapid Regeneration. Dark Sacrifice requires the Priest to remain above 70% health.
- Added conservative per-member, cooldown and channel tracking for shields, HoTs, Penance, Prayer of Mending, Power Infusion and healing racials.

### Restoration Shaman Healing Rotation

- Added Earth, Fire, Water and Air selectors for the Call of the Elements preset.
- Added verified Call of the Elements maintenance, with individual-totem fallback when the readable preset is empty, incomplete or mismatched.
- Added tracked Water Shield upkeep and Mana Tide Totem below 70% mana.
- Added per-member Riptide tracking with its 12-second cooldown and HoT-only effect.
- Added Chain Heal when its primary target has tracked Riptide and another dungeon-party member also needs healing.
- Added Lesser Healing Wave for smaller injuries and Healing Wave for larger injuries.
- Added Nature's Swiftness with Chain Heal for emergency group care, falling back to Healing Wave when Chain Heal is not learned.
- Added learned Blood Fury and Berserking to emergency healing.

### Holy Paladin Healing Rotation

- Added a Healing aura selector and maintenance for the selected learned aura.
- Added Blessing of Kings maintenance, with Greater Blessing of Kings used by class when learned.
- Added healer Seal maintenance and Judgement against a living enemy above 80% mana while a confirmed Seal is active.
- Added Flash of Light for routine care and Holy Shock for urgent instant healing.
- Limited Holy Light to emergency healing, a selected-friendly distress request or a confirmed Infusion of Light effect.
- Added Divine Favor into Holy Shock, or Holy Light when Holy Shock is unavailable.
- Added friendly-only Light's Vigil for two emergency injuries inside the eligible dungeon party.
- Added Oath of Light tank maintenance when the talent is learned and the option is enabled.
- Paladin racials remain manual.

### Developer support

- Added the `Documentation` folder with Leveling Rotation and HealingEngine provider contracts.
- Documented protected-value handling, spell registration, profile settings, Universal icon routing, HoT tracking and release validation.

## v1.1.5 Pre-launch Bluepost Hotfix

### Release scope and reliability

- Withdrew the unvalidated level-60 simulation presets and their class UI selectors from this release.
- Every class now defaults to **Leveling Rotation**. The Leveling checkbox cannot be left off, and old saved spec selections are cleared during profile normalization.
- Added mutually exclusive **DPS**, **Heals** and **Tank** rotation modes to supported class pages and the main-icon controls. DPS is the default; only roles supported by the current class are offered.
- A confirmed party role can select a supported mode automatically: Healer selects Heals and Tank selects Tank. A manual role selection is saved and takes priority; leaving an eligible group returns an automatically selected mode to DPS.
- The role controls use the same full-label and compact red/green presentation as the Burst and AoE controls.
- Debug reports now include the selected rotation role, whether it was chosen manually, the client-assigned group role and the exact Healing Rotation eligibility result.
- Preserved the finalized pre-simulation Leveling behavior inside the selected role. Tank-only threat recovery, taunts and tank stance/form priorities require Tank mode.
- Extended Leveling only with spells that are confirmed learned from the live spellbook. Talent abilities are never inferred from a build label.
- Limited Healing Rotation to Heals mode, a client-confirmed `HEALER` group role and a confirmed five-player `party` instance with two to five members. Raids, battlegrounds, arenas, world groups, solo play and protected or unknown role or instance states fall back to DPS Leveling Rotation.
- Limited all automatic healing rosters and multi-member heal checks to `party1` through `party4` plus the player.
- Healing recommendations select their party member through the focus pixel and mark every friendly healing decision with `castUnit = "focus"`. Healing macros use `[@mouseover,help,nodead]` for a manual override, then `[@focus,help,nodead]` for the engine-selected member, followed by the player fallback.
- Added a shared two-second spell-readability watchdog. After a readable `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT` attempt, a spell that produces no cast start, channel start or success is skipped and displays **“Spell Name blacklisted”** above the main icon.
- The target GUID and combat scope are captured with the original cast attempt, so changing targets during the two-second wait cannot move the blacklist. When the captured GUID is readable, the blacklist is stored for that enemy and remains if the player switches away and returns during the same fight. Protected target identity falls back to the current combat, and an out-of-combat attempt with no readable target uses a 30-second fallback. Encounter blacklists clear when combat ends or the player enters the world; a manual cast does not clear them early.
- A blacklisted spell is skipped by normal rotations, manual queues and Healing Rotation. Its Spells-tab row, icon, name and status turn red and show **BLACKLISTED**, while the user's enabled checkbox remains unchanged so an icon or binding problem is easy to locate.
- Normal GCD, range, movement, resource, terminal failure and interrupted-cast results do not trigger the blacklist. Auto-repeat and next-swing attacks keep their dedicated timing logic.
- Added learned-rank and localized-name mapping for every class so the watchdog and Leveling rotation use the same stable spell key.
- Hardened shared cooldown, target-cast, melee-range and combat-log identity checks so protected values fail closed instead of stopping a rotation with a taint error.

### Interface redesign

- Reorganized the complete options window around a fixed left navigation panel and a larger, fully scrollable content area.
- Added the **Nova Console** visual design as the standard ActionLITE appearance, with live Arcane, Frost, Fel, Blood, Royal Gold and SNES theme choices.
- Theme changes apply immediately and remain saved between sessions without reloading the interface.
- Added clearer section headers, tick-mark sliders, a wider scrollbar and grouped three-color header pixels.
- Added a transparent class portrait that follows the class profile selected for editing.
- Moved the complete theme editor into the main window so it cannot extend beyond the intended interface area.
- Increased the minimum options-window size so spell search, filters, blacklist status, icon assignments and class controls remain separated.
- Positioned DPS, Heals and Tank controls on the left of the main recommendation, with Burst and AoE centered on its right.
- Kept the borderless main recommendation, A icons and Cast Trail above full-screen warning effects without covering the protocol pixels.
- Opening ActionLITE during combat now goes directly to the Spells tab for immediate spell enable or disable changes.

## v1.1.6 Leveling, PvP and Interface Update

### Druid

- Caster Leveling opens with learned, ready Entangling Roots; otherwise it casts one Wrath before its damage-over-time spells, then continues with Wrath. The opener advances on confirmed casts and resets for a new target.
- Stabilized the caster opener so an active Entangling Roots cast cannot be replaced by Wrath after a transient movement or queue failure. The recommendation icon now clears when Roots or the opening Wrath begins, then stages Moonfire midway through Wrath so the reader cannot double-cast the opener.
- In-combat self-healing takes priority over damage at the existing caster and form emergency-health thresholds. Shift-out healing is allowed even when the live client reports the spell unusable in form.
- Learned Nature's Grasp protects an unshifted caster Druid when nearby enemies threaten them. Learned War Stomp is considered against two engaged attackers or a nearby casting enemy, including in Bear and Cat Form. Protected/unknown attacker information fails closed.
- Added a PvP cat stealth-opener choice for Pounce, Ravage or Shred against an attackable player, including duel opponents. The selected opener takes priority over the usual rotation, and PvP can use Burst priorities without changing the saved Burst toggle.

### Rogue

- Added separate main-hand and off-hand poison choices. Outside combat, the rotation checks temporary weapon enchants and recommends application only when the chosen poison is absent on an equipped weapon. A different recognized poison can be replaced; an unknown active enchant is left alone.
- Added **Apply Main-Hand Poison** and **Apply Off-Hand Poison** to the Spells tab. They default to **Universal7 Unit1** and **Universal10 Unit1** on A[6]. Their Template buttons provide copyable `/use` macros for slots 16 and 17; replace the item placeholder with the exact poison name and rank in your client's language, then bind the matching Unit1 icon in your pixel reader. Changing a poison selection requires updating its macro.
- Added a PvP stealth-opener choice, including Sap, Backstab, Cheap Shot, Ambush and Garrote. The chosen opener takes priority against an attackable player, and Burst priorities apply in PvP without changing the saved Burst toggle. Sap has target-scoped follow-up protection.
- After a successful Vanish, recommendations pause for two seconds. Shank's verified rank-one spell identity is used for localized learned-spell routing.

### Shaman

- Added an off-by-default **Earth Shock to tag a target** checkbox. When selected, a learned, ready and usable Earth Shock is suggested once before the normal pull sequence; a confirmed cast marks that enemy so the opener does not repeat during the fight.

### Warrior

- Corrected the Leveling AoE gate so Thunder Clap requires the selected number of confirmed nearby enemies. An unreadable or empty nameplate count now uses the single-target rotation instead of spending Rage on single-target Thunder Clap.
- Allowed Slam to use the same verified spell-range fallback as other melee attacks, and stopped Heroic Strike/Cleave from being recommended again while a next-swing strike is already queued.
- Deferred Slam while Heroic Strike or Cleave is queued for the next melee swing, and cleared the Slam recommendation during its cast to reduce repeat attempts.

### Shared spell routing and reliability

- Spell cooldown, usability, range and artwork checks now resolve the character's learned spell rank or localized client name for every class. Cast events and spellbook rescans normalize localized names back to stable rotation keys; Reckless Swings and Shank use verified Forever spell IDs.
- The failed-cast watchdog now also observes a stable, actually displayed A[3] or A[6] recommendation. If it remains immediately castable in combat for two seconds with no spellcast event, ActionLITE blacklists it for that enemy, alerts **“Spell Name blacklisted”** and highlights its Spells-tab row. It skips protected/ambiguous visuals, unknown range, unavailable spells, active casts or channels, and virtual poison actions. Bug reports now include the watchdog's reason and display state.
- The display-only path cannot tell whether a pixel reader was active or the player intentionally waited without pressing the icon. Please report an unexpected blacklist with an ActionLiteDebug capture so its scope and conditions can be checked.
- Bug reports now place a compact combat summary ahead of the full API report, so decisions, casts and relevant class settings remain visible when a long report is pasted into a size-limited message.

### Interface

- Aligned the DPS/Heals/Tank controls to thirds of the main icon and the full Burst/AoE controls to halves. Compact red/green indicators occupy thirds and align with the top and bottom of the icon.
- Softened the compact DPS/Heals/Tank and Burst/AoE indicators into circular, vignetted lights with muted green and red states.
- Added a theme-red **Profile Development** tab with a coming-soon message.


## v1.1.7 Class Rotation Update

### Warrior
- Rend now refreshes after its tracked duration on targets that survive beyond the opener, including fights where target health remains protected.
- Added **Use Demoralizing Shout in AoE only**. When enabled, Demoralizing Shout requires at least two confirmed nearby engaged enemies in DPS or Tank mode.
- Added **Disable automatic targeting / Charge opener** to Tank settings. It suppresses automatic Attack/Charge engagement and in-combat Charge so the player can start each pull manually.

### Druid
- Corrected **Bash** interrupt priority in Bear and Tank rotations. It now stays enabled for existing profiles unless explicitly disabled, and still requires a detected enemy cast and confirmed melee range.
- Tightened Demoralizing Roar positioning with confirmed melee range and a four-yard safety margin when distance is readable.

### Rogue
- Added **Eureka!** to the spell menu and Burst priority for learned Gnome Rogues.
- Added **Escape Artist** to the spell menu and control logic. It is suggested only for a positively detected root or snare.
- Corrected Shank Energy headroom handling so a Shank that would waste its Energy gain falls through to the normal builder or finisher instead of leaving the rotation on the pooling icon.
- Added protected target-cast and channel event tracking so Kick remains available when Forever hides `UnitCastingInfo` during combat.

### Shaman
- Added a mutually exclusive **Flame Shock opener** option beside **Earth Shock to tag a target**. Either opener may be selected, or both may remain disabled.

### Hunter
- Added a dedicated **Use Multi-Shot in AoE** control, enabled by default.
- Multi-Shot is now evaluated in every Hunter Leveling AoE path with the selected confirmed-enemy threshold; its current instant Forever behavior no longer inherits Volley's stationary requirement.
