# ActionLITE v1.1.0 Release Notes

Version 1.1.0 | October 30, 2026

## HealingEngine framework

- Added base healing ruleset for **Enable Healing Rotation** to healing classes.
- Added the WoW Forever HealingEngine foundation for Druid, Paladin, Priest and Shaman.
- Added protected friendly-unit selection through the Action-compatible healing target pixel.
- Added predicted-health visual masks so future healing spell recommendations and party targets share the same client-evaluated condition.
- Added nested protected masks for healing decisions that require a second injured member plus a mana condition, without exposing protected health or mana to Lua.
- Added readable roster, role, friendly, connection, visibility, death and confirmed spell-range eligibility checks.
- Added integrated HealingEngine state to ActionLiteDebug reports.
- Added a selected-friendly distress rule that immediately recommends the strongest available normal direct heal and its matching focus pixel.
- Added grouped healer damage rules: timed Shoot for wand users, or periodic damage above 80% mana for non-wand users.
- Added a shared five-second mana rule for all healer classes. Successful healing/support casts pause Shoot and damage-over-time recommendations so Spirit regeneration can begin; healing still occurs when a configured health threshold is met.
- Healing recommendations take priority over all damage recommendations and return to damage after predicted healing is sufficient.
- Solo characters bypass Healing Rotation and continue using their selected leveling or damage rotation.

## Healer class controls

- Added **Enable Healing Rotation**, disabled by default, to each healer class UI.
- Added **Initiate healing at HP**, default 80%, for efficient heals and HoTs.
- Added **Emergency healing**, default 30%, for the largest heals and cooldowns.
- Known DPS party/raid members use both healing thresholds at 10 percentage points below the selected values, keeping tank and healer care at higher priority.
- Disabling Healing Rotation clears party/raid targeting and keeps the existing leveling, damage and self-healing rotations unchanged.

## Druid Healing Rotation

- Added tracked Regrowth, Rejuvenation and Healing Touch priorities for group healing.
- Added an immediate Rejuvenation follow-up after Healing Touch or Regrowth when Rejuvenation is not already tracked on that member.
- Added emergency Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Healing Touch and Elune's Light priorities. Elune's Light is reserved for emergency healing.
- Added tracked tank Thorns at the start of combat.
- Added out-of-combat Mark of the Wild for a selected friendly group member, followed by health top-off above 80% mana while not eating or drinking.
- Added timed Faerie Fire for selected elite enemies and timed Moonfire above 80% mana.
- Added five-second-rule protection so healing follow-ups and mana recovery are not interrupted by a damage recommendation.
- Added Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Tranquility and Wild Growth to the Druid spell menu for learned-spell detection, Universal icons and manual queues.
- Dispels, Entangling Roots and Hibernate remain manual. Tranquility and Wild Growth remain manually queued until multiple injured group members can be identified safely.

## Priest Healing Rotations

- Added mutually exclusive **Discipline Priest** and **Holy Priest** checkboxes beneath the shared healing controls.
- All Priest healing modes use timed Shoot against a selected living enemy in combat and respect the shared five-second mana rule after healing.
- Discipline tracks Power Word: Shield per party member, maintains the tank shield, shields injured members before filler heals, and uses Penance at 20 points below the initiate-healing slider.
- Discipline uses learned Binding Heal instead of Flash Heal, casts Power Infusion only on eligible caster DPS, and reserves automatic Prayer of Healing for the Inner Focus emergency sequence.
- Holy tracks Renew per party member, uses Heal for smaller injuries, and uses learned Binding Heal instead of Flash Heal for larger injuries.
- Holy emergency healing prefers learned Prayer of Mending, then Inner Focus with Greater Heal.
- Direct tank heals retain priority so critical heals can trigger the passive Inspiration talent. Inspiration is not treated as a castable spell.
- Added Greater Heal, Flash Heal, Binding Heal, Prayer of Mending and Power Infusion to the Priest spell menu for learned-spell detection, Universal icons and manual queues.
- Added automatic learned Priest racials to both healing profiles: Human Divine Grace on an injured friendly target; Night Elf Elune's Light, Gnome Eureka!, Undead Dark Sacrifice and Troll Berserking during emergency healing; and Troll Rapid Regeneration for emergency self-healing.
- Added protected player-health gating so Dark Sacrifice is only recommended while the Priest is above 70% health.
- Added fallback cooldown tracking for every healing racial and six-second channel protection for Rapid Regeneration. Divine Grace also clears the tracked shield reapplication lock on its recipient.
- Added Divine Grace and Rapid Regeneration to the Priest spell menu for learned-spell detection, Universal icons and manual queues.

## Restoration Shaman Healing Rotation

- Added four Call of the Elements selectors beneath the shared healing sliders for Earth, Fire, Water and Air totems.
- Added tracked Call of the Elements preset maintenance. The selected values tell ActionLITE which saved preset to verify; the same totems must be configured in WoW's protected Totem Bar preset. A readable empty, incomplete or mismatched preset falls back to individual selected totems instead of repeatedly casting Call.
- Added tracked Water Shield upkeep and automatic Mana Tide Totem below 70% mana. Mana Tide temporarily owns the Water slot, then Call of the Elements restores the selected Water totem after Mana Tide expires.
- Added per-member Riptide tracking with its six-second cooldown and 15-second effect window.
- Added Chain Heal group logic when its primary target still has tracked Riptide, at least one other member is below the appropriate healing threshold, and Shaman mana is above 50%.
- Added Lesser Healing Wave for smaller injuries and Healing Wave for larger injuries, with tank priority for opportunities to trigger the passive Ancestral Healing armor effect.
- Added emergency Blood Fury and Berserking, followed by a tracked Nature's Swiftness and Chain Heal sequence. Healing Wave is used when Chain Heal has not been learned.
- Added timed Flame Shock for the grouped healer damage rule above 80% mana, with the shared five-second mana rule preventing damage casts from interrupting regeneration.
- Added Call of the Elements, Chain Heal, Mana Tide Totem, Nature's Swiftness and Riptide to the Shaman spell menu for learned-spell detection, Universal icons and manual queues.

## Developer support

- Added the `Documentation` folder with the v1.1.0 combat-rotation and HealingEngine provider contracts.
- Documented protected-value rules, spell registration, settings, Universal icon routing, HoT tracking and validation.

Additional class-specific party-healing spell priorities will be added through the shared HealingEngine provider framework.
