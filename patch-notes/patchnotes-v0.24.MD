# ActionLITE v0.24 Patch Notes

## Wands

Wand timing corrected for all wand users.

## Shaman

- Corrected Flame Shock duration tracking for Call of Flame: three additional seconds per talent point, up to 21 seconds.
- Improved duration tracking after talent changes and while in combat.

## Priest

- Corrected Devouring Plague cooldown tracking to 24 seconds.
- Corrected Vampiric Embrace cooldown tracking to 30 seconds.

## Spell Menu Additions

- Mage: Frost Blast.
- Paladin: Salvation, Mass Salvation, and Revoke.

These spells support Universal icon assignment and manual queuing when learned.
